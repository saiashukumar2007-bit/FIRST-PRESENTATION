POPULAR TOURIST PLACES

Discover the natural beauty, spirituality and heritage of Dhenkanal.

TEMPLE

Kapilash Temple

Kapilash Temple is one of the most famous religious destinations of Dhenkanal. Located among beautiful green hills, the temple is dedicated to Lord Shiva and attracts devotees and tourists throughout the year.

SPIRITUAL

Joranda Mahima Gadi

Joranda Mahima Gadi is an important spiritual centre associated with Mahima Dharma. Its peaceful surroundings and unique religious traditions make it a special destination.

HERITAGE

Dhenkanal Palace

Dhenkanal Palace represents the royal heritage of the region. Its architecture and historical character make it an interesting destination for visitors who love history and culture.

HISTORY

Jatan Nagar Palace

Jatan Nagar Palace is a historic attraction surrounded by natural beauty. The site offers visitors an opportunity to explore the history and heritage of the region.

HERITAGE

Anantasayi Vishnu

Anantasayi Vishnu at Sarang is a remarkable religious and historical attraction. The site is known for its representation of Lord Vishnu and its cultural importance.

NATURE

Sapua Dam

Sapua Dam is surrounded by greenery and peaceful landscapes. The reservoir creates a relaxing environment and is a beautiful destination for nature lovers.

NATURE

Dandadhar Dam

Dandadhar Dam is a scenic destination surrounded by greenery and natural landscapes. It is a peaceful place to enjoy the beauty of nature.

TEMPLE

Astashambhu Temple

Astashambhu Temple at Kualo is an important religious attraction associated with Lord Shiva. It is a peaceful destination for devotees and visitors interested in Odisha's heritage.

WATERFALL

Sunajhar Waterfall

Sunajhar Waterfall is a beautiful natural attraction surrounded by greenery and rocky landscapes. It is an enjoyable destination for nature lovers and photography.
